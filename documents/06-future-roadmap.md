# Future Roadmap

This document outlines the long-term vision and planned improvements for Nevyrion Learning beyond the Academic MVP.

The features listed below are intentionally excluded from the first release to keep the MVP focused,
maintainable, and achievable within the available development time.

---

## Product Vision

Nevyrion Learning aims to become a comprehensive learning ecosystem
that combines structured education, community engagement, and modern learning experiences into a single platform.

---

# Version 2

### Community Posts

Allow learners to share their learning journey, challenges, tips, and experiences to help other learners avoid common mistakes and stay motivated.

---

### Nev Coins Reward System

Reward top-performing learners with Nev Coins that can be exchanged for discounts on future courses.

---

### Learning Events

Allow learners to join educational events, workshops, and community sessions.

---

### Instructor Applications

Allow users to apply as instructors instead of being manually created by administrators.

---

### Course Leaderboards

Display course rankings based on learner performance to encourage healthy competition among students.

---

### Daily Motivation

Display motivational quotes or learning tips to encourage learners to stay consistent.

---

### Advanced Gamification

Introduce experience points, achievements, badges, streaks, and additional reward systems beyond Nev Coins.

---

### Instructor & Student Messaging

Allow instructors and students to communicate securely within the platform without relying on external social media platforms.

---

# Version 3

### Personalized Course Recommendations

Recommend courses based on learner interests, completed courses, and learning progress.

---

### Learning Analytics

Provide advanced learning analytics and insights for both students and instructors.

---

### Course Wishlist Sharing

Allow learners to share their wishlist with friends or on social media.

---

### AI Tutor

Provide AI-powered learning assistance and personalized learning guidance.

---

### Live Classes

Support live interactive classes between instructors and students.

---

### Mobile Application

Provide native mobile applications for Android and iOS.

---

### Course Marketplace

Allow approved instructors to publish and sell their own courses directly through the platform.
