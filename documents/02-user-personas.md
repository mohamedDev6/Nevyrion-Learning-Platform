# User Personas

This document describes the primary user personas of Nevyrion Learning.

The personas represent the main types of users the platform is designed for.

---

# Guest

## Description

A visitor exploring the platform before creating an account.

## Goals

- Explore available courses.
- Discover learning paths.
- Evaluate instructors.
- Decide whether to register.

## Pain Points

- Unsure whether the platform meets their needs.
- Wants to evaluate course quality before enrolling.

## Needs

- Easy navigation.
- Search and filtering.
- Course previews.
- Clear learning paths.

---

# Student

## Description

A learner who wants to study programming through structured learning paths and high-quality courses.

## Goals

- Learn programming efficiently.
- Follow a clear roadmap.
- Track learning progress.
- Earn certificates.

## Pain Points

- Information overload.
- Lack of structure.
- Losing motivation.
- Difficulty choosing what to learn next.

## Needs

- Organized learning paths.
- Progress tracking.
- Resume learning.
- Quizzes and challenges.
- Certificates.

---

# Instructor

## Description

An educator who creates programming courses and monitors student progress.

## Goals

- Publish high-quality courses.
- Teach effectively.
- Monitor student performance.
- Improve course quality.

## Pain Points

- Managing educational content.
- Tracking student engagement.
- Maintaining course quality.

## Needs

- Course management tools.
- Student analytics.
- Quiz management.
- Progress reports.
- Course review workflow.

---

# Administrator

## Description

The platform administrator responsible for maintaining quality, managing users, and overseeing platform operations.

## Goals

- Maintain platform quality.
- Review submitted courses.
- Manage users and instructors.
- Ensure smooth platform operation.

## Pain Points

- Reviewing large amounts of content.
- Managing platform resources.
- Maintaining educational standards.

## Needs

- User management.
- Course approval tools.
- Learning path management.
- Platform analytics.
- Content moderation.
